**Lists of experiments we need (MUST need fact checking).** <br>
<br> - _Regolith Analysis:_ Regolith refers to the layers of soil, dust, and other weathered rocks covering the marsain bedrock. A regolith test would require you to collect soil from locations, keep tabs on where each sample came from, and analyse its mineral and chemical composition. It also needs you to compare samples from different sites. The equipments (allegedly) needed are sterile sample bags/containers, scoop or corer, sieves, balance, microscope, spectrometer/chemical analyzer, labels. <br>
<br> - _Basic Atmospheric Monitoring:_ You gt continuously measure atmospheric pressure, temperature, humidity, wind, dust concentration, and gas composition. You need a pressure sensor, thermometers, humidity sensor, anemometer, gas analyzer, dust sensor, data logger. This part is fact checked. <br>
<br>- _Radiation exposure test:_ They place dosimeters (a device that measures how much radiation an object has absorbed) inside and outside the habitat and in different shielding configurations. Record radiation over an extended period and compare the readings. You need dosimeters, radiation spectrometer, shielding materials, data logger <br>
