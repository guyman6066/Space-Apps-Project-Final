Outline of Ideas:
- Team of 4 sent to Mars to conduct experiments for 13-16 months.
- Limited food (4380 pounds, 1095 per person), water (2,200 gallons, 550 gallons per person), oxygen (3,888 pounds, 972 per person) and electricity is provided.
- Players have to ration resources while assigning different crew members to different tasks, ie. growing plants, collecting samples, expanding the base etc..
- When research points are collected, the crew members have to read through it and determine whether it is sound enough to keep or discard.
- The end goal would be to take all gathered information regarding whether Mars is inhabitable or not and the player is given a grade based on what quality research they submit every time. (quota system)

- Players are given a set number of structures that they can build, and each construction takes 1-5 days to build depending on scale - Lore reason: Could be there in the format of kits on the ship to save space.
- If a member's hunger/thirst/tiredness reaches 100%, the game ends.

- Frequent dust storms. If a crew member is assigned to study the weather patterns, they will know of dust storms for the next 5 hours and players will be warned 5 minutes (IRL) before the storm hits, if the forecast is not studied, players are warned 30 seconds before the storm hits.
- Dust storms damage outdoor experiments, and if a certain type of apparatus is destroyed, that experiment can no longer be done (permanently inaccessible for the rest of the playthrough). Apparatus can be picked up and stored indoors in case of a dust storm.
